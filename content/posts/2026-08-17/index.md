# $DGAI Airdrop Claim Is Now Live

The $DGAI airdrop eligibility check and claim process is now officially live through the official DGrid portal.

🔗 **Claim Portal:** [https://dgrid.ai/airdrop](https://dgrid.ai/airdrop)

## Key Dates

- Claim Closes: August 22, at 8:00 UTC
- $DGAI Distribution: On TGE day, directly to the bound exchange account

During the claim window, eligible users will be able to:

- Check their airdrop eligibility
- Review their $DGAI allocation
- Choose from multiple claim options, including staking pathways with rewards
- Bind a supported exchange account through the official DGrid process

## Airdrop Eligibility

Eligibility and allocation will be determined based on DGrid’s official records, including user activity, points records, campaign participation, and anti-Sybil checks.

Eligible user categories include:

- **DGrid Premium Members:** Mined tDGAI will be converted into $DGAI airdrops at a 1:1 ratio.
- **DGrid Product Users and AI Arena Point Holders**
- **DGrid Galxe Participants:** Including the Top300 on the Starboard and participants from other DGrid Galxe quests.
- **Early DGrid Core Contributors**

## Claim Options

Eligible users may choose one of the following allocation plans during the claim window:

### 180-Day Staking Plan

- 10% available at TGE
- 90% staked for 180 days
- Estimated annualized reward rate: 60%

### 360-Day Staking Plan

- 20% available at TGE
- 80% staked for 360 days
- Estimated annualized reward rate: 90%

### No-Staking Plan

- 30% available at TGE
- 70% permanently burned

## Important Reminders

- Please complete your claim selection and exchange account binding within the official claim window.
- If no staking plan is selected before the deadline, your allocation will automatically enter the 360-day staking plan.
- For users who choose the 180-day or 360-day staking plan, please note that unstaking must be initiated manually after TGE. The unstaking countdown will begin once your request is submitted.
- To receive the available $DGAI portion on TGE day, please bind a supported exchange account through the official DGrid process and carefully confirm all information before submission.
- Premium tDGAI mining has been paused and will resume with $DGAI at a later stage, while tDGAI transfers have also been disabled.
- Once the claim window closes, missed or incomplete submissions may not be reopened.
