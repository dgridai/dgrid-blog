DGrid is teaming up with Sahara AI to support developers and users building with AI in Web3. Sahara AI’s Tools-as-a-Service brings market data, project research, market analysis, and real-time crypto intelligence into AI models and MCP servers. DGrid complements these capabilities with low-cost, verifiable, and trustless access to models and AI agents through one API.

The collaboration combines access to crypto intelligence with the inference infrastructure needed to put it to use.

## A Simpler Way to Build

Sahara AI’s tools help applications work with relevant market information. DGrid’s unified API simplifies model access, while intelligent routing considers task requirements, performance, and cost when handling requests.

These complementary services can support research assistants and market monitoring agents, helping developers connect information with model reasoning while reducing the work of managing multiple providers.

## Connecting Builders with Opportunity

The partnership also creates opportunities for shared developer support and product showcases across both communities. Practical examples can help builders understand how Sahara’s tools and DGrid’s model services fit into their applications, while giving emerging products greater exposure across Web3.

Working with those teams will help both sides identify useful integration opportunities around actual development needs.

## Growing Through Real Applications

As the collaboration develops, DGrid and Sahara AI will explore closer connections between tools, models, and agent workflows. Developer feedback will guide that work, with a focus on making crypto intelligence easier to use in applications that people rely on.
